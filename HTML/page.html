ABOUT / THE JOURNAL

We travel to
pay attention.

Sierra Madre is an independent field journal about landscapes, movement, memory and the people who understand a place by living inside it.

ESTABLISHED / LUZON / 2026
01 / PURPOSE

Not a guide to where to go.
A record of how it felt
to move through.

We publish journeys, photo essays, conversations and observations from roads, mountains, coasts and the places between them.

Our stories begin with geography but remain for the human details: a meal shared, a route remembered, the color of weather arriving.

02 / OUR PRACTICE

Field
principles

  1. 01

    Move slowly

    Time changes what a place is willing to show.

  2. 02

    Listen first

    Local knowledge leads every story we make.

  3. 03

    Leave lightly

    Attention carries a responsibility to the landscape.

  4. 04

    Stay curious

    The overlooked detail is often the beginning.
